## Privacy Policy

### Zplit 

Zplit is built on a privacy-first, local-first philosophy. By design, Zplit does not operate any central servers, does not collect your personal data, and does not transmit your information to any third party. All data lives on your device.

### 1. Who We Are

Zplit is a free and open-source application for decentralized expense management. It is developed and maintained by the Zplit open-source contributors under AOSSIE. The app is available on Android via the Google Play Store. Because Zplit is a local-first, offline-capable application, we have no visibility into your data whatsoever.

### 2. Data We Collect — And What We Don't

Zplit does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any government-issued ID. , expense amounts, and financial balances are never sent to us. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data. We do not collect behavioral analytics, usage metrics, or crash reports. We do not collect payment information or bank account details of any kind.

All data you enter into Zplit —  expense records, amounts, categories, and balances — is stored exclusively on your device using local encrypted storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it with another Zplit user through one of the peer-to-peer transfer methods described below.

### 3. Device Permissions

**Camera** — used for scanning QR codes to join groups or sync data, and for capturing receipt images for OCR processing. All image processing is done entirely on-device using Google ML Kit. Receipt images are never uploaded to any server.

**Bluetooth** — used for discovering nearby Zplit users and syncing expense data peer-to-peer. No data passes through our servers at any point during this process.

**Wi-Fi and Local Network** — used for Wi-Fi Direct connections for high-speed device-to-device data sync. No internet connection is required or used during this process.

**NFC** — used for tap-to-share group invites and expense data. NFC communication is strictly device-to-device and does not involve any network transmission.

**Storage and Files** — used for saving and reading the local encrypted database, and for exporting backup files that you explicitly request.

### 4. Peer-to-Peer Data Transfer

When you use Zplit's sync features via Bluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct, NFC, or QR code, data is transmitted directly between your device and the recipient's device. This transfer is encrypted end-to-end using cryptographic signatures. It does not route through Zplit's servers or any third-party cloud. It requires explicit action by you such as tapping Share or scanning a QR code, and it only transfers the specific group or expense data you choose to share. You are responsible for ensuring you share data only with trusted individuals.

### 5. On-Device OCR and AI Processing

When you use the receipt scanning feature, the image is processed entirely on your device. No image or extracted text is ever transmitted to any external server. The extracted merchant name, amount, and date are pre-filled into the expense form for your review. You retain full control and can discard, edit, or confirm any OCR result before it is saved to your local database.

### 6. Third-Party Services

Zplit does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services. The only third-party component used is Google ML Kit, which operates fully on-device and does not send any data to Google's servers.

### 7. Data Security

All local data is stored in an encrypted database on your device. All peer-to-peer data transfers use end-to-end encrypted channels with cryptographic signatures to prevent tampering or spoofing. Optional backup exports can be protected with a user-defined passphrase. We encourage you to keep your device and operating system updated and to use a strong screen lock.

### 8. Your Rights and Controls

All your data is viewable within the app at any time. You can edit any expense  You can delete individual expenses or uninstall the app entirely

### 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notification, a notice on the Play Store listing, and an updated version in our public GitHub repository.

### 10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach the Zplit contributors via our public GitHub repository at [github.com/StabilityNexus/Zplit](https://github.com/StabilityNexus/Zplit).
